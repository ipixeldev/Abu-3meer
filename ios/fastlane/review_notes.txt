Abu 3meer supports Arabic and English.

Authentication and account controls:
- Sign in with Apple is available on iOS as an equivalent option alongside Google and email.
- Account deletion is available in Settings > Account > Delete Account.
- Email and password changes are available in Settings > Account for accounts using email/password authentication.
- YouTube membership does not use Google or YouTube OAuth. A user may submit a stable YouTube `UC...` channel ID or `/channel/UC...` URL as an untrusted claim; submitting or typing a channel never grants membership.
- A moderator, admin, or super admin must review that claim, and approval is accepted only when the exact channel ID is present in the latest unexpired complete YouTube Studio membership export. Staff decisions are audited and one approved channel cannot be attached to multiple app accounts.
- The export is uploaded through the authenticated app API as UTF-8 CSV/TSV. The raw file and YouTube display names are not retained. Present approved channel IDs are active; IDs missing from the next complete export become lapsed. Because YouTube provides no automatic export or cancellation webhook, the staff screen shows when the latest list is stale.
- Pending, rejected, lapsed, and non-member accounts retain all normal fan functionality.

XP and rankings:
- The app is completely free. It has no purchases, paid entry, betting, wagering, prizes, rewards, or leaderboard winners.
- Users receive 50 XP once at signup, 5 XP for the first login each UTC day, and XP for correct football predictions and correct video-question or player-guess answers.
- Verified YouTube channel members receive 2× XP only on eligible prediction and video-answer actions. Signup and daily-login XP are never doubled.
- XP has no monetary value, cannot be bought, transferred, or redeemed, and unlocks nothing.
- Current-month, previous-month, and season leaderboards show fan progress for recognition only. Monthly totals reset while the previous month is preserved, and completed seasons remain available as archived rankings.
- XP and leaderboard rules are available in Settings > Legal & Privacy and at https://ipixeldev.github.io/Abu-3meer/competition-rules/.
- Apple is not a sponsor of, involved in, or responsible for any competition.

Data and content behavior:
- Location permission is optional and is used once to suggest a country; users can select a country manually.
- Match facts, lineups, tables, and results appear only when the official football data provider publishes them.
- Notification permission is optional.

Legal and support pages are available in Settings and at https://ipixeldev.github.io/Abu-3meer/.
