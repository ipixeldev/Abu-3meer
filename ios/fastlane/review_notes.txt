Response to App Review — Guideline 2.1

Moderation path: Home > View Full Leaderboard > another fan profile > Report Profile or Block User. Unblock at avatar > Settings > Account > Blocked users.

2. Purpose and target audience
ABU 3MEER is an Arabic/English football fan app for ages 13+. It offers football information/videos, free predictions/questions, recognition-only XP rankings, notifications, and optional Members content. There is no paid entry, betting, wagering, prize, or redeemable reward. XP has no monetary value and cannot be transferred or redeemed.

3. Setup and access
No VPN, invitation, sample file, or special setup is required; the production backend is live. Apple, Google, and email sign-in are supported. Current credentials are in App Review Information > Sign-in Information. Photo/location access is optional and country can be selected manually. Tabs: Home, Predict, Challenges, Members, Profile. Account deletion: avatar > Settings > Account > Delete account.

4. External services and platforms
Apple/Google/Firebase Authentication: sign-in. FCM/APNs: optional notifications. StoreKit/App Store and RevenueCat: purchases, entitlements, restore, and management. Google Mobile Ads/UMP: one non-personalized Home banner for non-members only. api.abu3meer.com, PostgreSQL, Redis, and Cloudflare: backend and media. API-Football/TheSportsDB: football data. YouTube public feeds/profile data: videos and comparison of a submitted public profile link with a staff-uploaded membership list; no YouTube authorization or purchase. OpenStreetMap Nominatim/ipapi.co: optional country suggestion with manual choice. No AI service provides a core function.

5. Regional differences
Core features, rules, benefits, and purchase methods are consistent across regions. Users choose Arabic or English. Apple localizes prices/taxes; football/video availability follows source publication.

6. Regulation, moderation, and third-party content
The app is not a betting, gambling, financial, medical, or other regulated service and claims no club/league affiliation. It displays factual football data and team/league identifiers from configured data services and links to public content from the configured ABU 3MEER YouTube channel. The submission declares third-party content. Please ask in Resolution Center if documentation is needed for a specific provider, channel, image, or identifier.

User-selected names may appear in public fan profiles/leaderboards. Optional profile photos are not public in this build. Profile text is safety-checked. Users can Report, Block, Unblock, and contact support@abu3meer.com; authorized staff review reports.

7. In-App Purchase
Navigate: sign in > Members > View plans. The paywall shows title, duration, localized price, renewal terms, benefits, Restore Purchases, Terms, and Privacy.

- Ostoora3, product ID Ostoora3: one month.
- Ostoora3 Pro Max, product ID Ostoora3_Pro_Max: one year.

Both unlock the same Members content and badge; only billing period differs. Benefits include 150 recognition XP on first activation, 50 XP per verified renewal, and 2x XP only on eligible correct predictions. Rankings have no prizes. All iOS purchases use Apple IAP; there is no external purchase link. Restore and Manage Subscription are under Members. The deletion dialog warns that account deletion does not cancel Apple billing and links to Apple's subscription management.

An existing Abu 3meer YouTube channel member may separately submit that user's public channel-profile link for comparison with the staff-uploaded list. This cannot buy, renew, cancel, or manage YouTube membership. Equivalent access is sold through Apple IAP; free features require no purchase.
