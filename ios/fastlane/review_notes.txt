Response to App Review — Guidelines 5.1.2(i), 5.1.1(v), and 2.1

CORRECTED BUILD 35

ATT / ADVERTISING PRIVACY
On a fresh installation, launch the app and remain on the Home screen. If a regional Google UMP consent form is required, make a choice and dismiss that form. Immediately afterward, iOS presents Apple's system "Allow ABU 3MEER to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites?" App Tracking Transparency prompt. Where no regional form is required, the ATT prompt appears directly after launch. Build 35 waits for ATT to complete before Google Mobile Ads is initialized or any ad is requested. The app remains fully usable if permission is denied and continues to request restricted, non-personalized ads. App Privacy now discloses tracking and Device ID as Data Used to Track You. Ad privacy choices can later be reviewed under Settings > Legal & Privacy > Ad privacy choices when required in the user's region.

ACCOUNT DELETION
For every signed-in account: bottom navigation > Profile > ACCOUNT SETTINGS & DELETE ACCOUNT (directly below the fan card) > Account > red Delete account row. Type DELETE and, for email/password, enter the current password. The red DELETE ACCOUNT button becomes enabled. Confirming permanently deletes profile data, activity, device registrations, and the Firebase sign-in account, then returns to Guest/signed-out state. Apple/Google accounts use provider reauthentication. The subscription warning does not block deletion.

Please do not delete the reusable App Review demo account because its credentials would stop working. The physical-device recording below demonstrates creating an account and completing permanent deletion through confirmation:

APP PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
ABU 3MEER is an Arabic/English football fan app for ages 13+. It provides football information/videos, free predictions/questions, recognition-only XP rankings, notifications, and optional Members content. There is no paid entry, wagering, prize, or redeemable reward; XP has no monetary value.

SETUP AND ACCESS
No special setup is required. Apple, Google, and email sign-in are supported. Reusable credentials are in App Review Information. Photo/location are optional; country can be selected manually. Tabs: Home, Predict, Challenges, Members, Profile.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Apple/Google/Firebase: sign-in. FCM/APNs: notifications. StoreKit and RevenueCat: purchases/entitlements. Google Mobile Ads/UMP: ads/consent. api.abu3meer.com, PostgreSQL, Redis, Cloudflare: backend/media. API-Football/TheSportsDB: football data. YouTube public feeds/profile data: videos and membership-list comparison, without YouTube authorization or purchase. Nominatim/ipapi.co: optional country suggestion. No AI service is core.

REGIONS AND CONTENT
Core features, rules, benefits, and purchase methods are consistent across regions. Users choose Arabic or English. Apple localizes prices/taxes; football/video availability follows source publication. The app is not a betting, gambling, financial, medical, or regulated service and claims no club/league affiliation. It displays factual football data and links to public content from the configured ABU 3MEER YouTube channel. Users can report, block, unblock, and contact support@abu3meer.com; authorized staff review reports.

IN-APP PURCHASE
Navigate: sign in > Members > View plans. Ostoora3 (product Ostoora3) is monthly; Ostoora3 Pro Max (product Ostoora3_Pro_Max) is yearly. Both unlock the same Members content and badge; only billing period differs. The paywall shows localized price, duration, renewal terms, benefits, Restore Purchases, Terms, and Privacy. All iOS purchases use Apple IAP; there is no external purchase link. Account deletion does not cancel Apple billing and links to Apple's subscription management.
