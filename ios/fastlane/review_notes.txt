Abu 3meer supports Arabic and English. This is the full app, not a standalone paywall demonstration.

Subscription review:
- Sign in, then open Profile or Settings and select View plans. The Members tab also provides access to membership plans.
- Ostoora3 (product ID: Ostoora3) is an auto-renewable monthly subscription. Ostoora3 Pro Max (product ID: Ostoora3_Pro_Max) is an auto-renewable yearly subscription. Both unlock the same member benefits; only the billing period differs.
- The paywall shows App Store-localized prices and billing periods before purchase. RevenueCat manages the native purchase flow. The backend verifies the abu_3meer_pro entitlement for member access.
- Restore purchases and subscription management are available in the subscription panel. Subscribers receive access to Members, eligible member bonuses, and a green subscriber badge beside their names.
- Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through the App Store. Deleting an app account does not cancel its App Store subscription.

Authentication and account controls:
- Sign in with Apple is available on iOS as an equivalent option alongside Google and email.
- Account deletion is available in Settings > Account > Delete Account.
- Email and password changes are available in Settings > Account for accounts using email/password authentication.
- The separate YouTube membership check accepts a channel profile link entered by the user. The API compares its channel ID with the current complete membership CSV/TSV uploaded by staff. This is a list match, not proof that the user owns the channel. It does not request Google/YouTube OAuth permission.
- A moderator, admin, or super admin uploads the complete YouTube Studio current-members export through the authenticated app API as UTF-8 CSV/TSV. This file is the membership authority. One channel cannot be attached to multiple app accounts.
- IDs present in the latest unexpired complete export are active; IDs missing from the next complete export become lapsed. Because YouTube provides no automatic export or cancellation webhook, the staff screen shows when the latest list is stale. No creator-members API or restricted membership scope is used.
- Pending, rejected, lapsed, and non-member accounts retain all normal fan functionality.

XP and rankings:
- Predictions and video questions have no entry fee, stake, wager, or cash prize. Subscriptions are optional and do not buy XP.
- Users receive 50 XP once at signup, 5 XP for the first login each UTC day, and XP for correct football predictions and correct video-question or player-guess answers.
- Eligible members receive the configured member bonus only on eligible prediction and video-answer actions. Signup and daily-login XP are never doubled.
- XP has no monetary value and cannot be bought, transferred, or redeemed.
- Current-month, previous-month, and season leaderboards show fan progress for recognition only. Monthly totals reset while the previous month is preserved, and completed seasons remain available as archived rankings.
- XP and leaderboard rules are available in Settings > Legal & Privacy and at https://ipixeldev.github.io/Abu-3meer/competition-rules/.

Data and content behavior:
- Location permission is optional and is used once to suggest a country; users can select a country manually.
- Match facts, lineups, tables, and results appear only when the official football data provider publishes them.
- Notification permission is optional.
- Public channel uploads are shown on Home, separately from members-only content in Members.

Legal and support pages are available in Settings and at https://ipixeldev.github.io/Abu-3meer/.
