Abu 3meer brings football predictions, video challenges, exclusive content, and fan progression together in one app.

• Match predictions: Predict the score, winner, and first scorer before kickoff.
• Match center: Follow fixtures, results, match facts, lineups, and league tables when the official data provider publishes them.
• Video challenges: Answer questions from Abu 3meer videos and collect hidden Player Cards.
• Exclusive videos: Watch special video releases made available to app users.
• Points and progress: Build your activity streak, unlock achievements, and climb monthly, seasonal, and all-time leaderboards.
• Rewards: Spend earned loyalty points on available rewards.
• YouTube membership: Verified channel members receive 2× points on predictions and video challenges.

Choose Arabic or English and control notifications for match reminders, new challenges, exclusive videos, prediction results, and important announcements.
