Abu 3meer brings football predictions, video questions, exclusive content, and fan progression together in one completely free app.

• Match predictions: Predict the score, match outcome, and first scorer before kickoff.
• Match center: Follow fixtures, results, match facts, lineups, and league tables when the official data provider publishes them.
• Video questions: Answer questions from Abu 3meer videos and guess featured players.
• Exclusive videos: Watch special video releases made available to app users.
• XP and rankings: Receive 50 XP once at signup, 5 XP for the first login each UTC day, and XP for correct football predictions and correct video-question or player-guess answers. Compare fan progress on current-month, previous-month, and season leaderboards.
• YouTube membership: Verified channel members receive 2× XP on eligible prediction and video-question actions only. Signup and daily-login XP are not doubled.

Choose Arabic or English and control notifications for match reminders, new questions, exclusive videos, prediction results, and important announcements.

There are no purchases, paid entry, betting, wagering, prizes, rewards, or leaderboard winners. XP has no monetary value, cannot be bought, transferred, or redeemed, and unlocks nothing. Leaderboards provide recognition only.
