Age suitability

Designed for fans aged 13+

Abu 3meer contains football predictions, trivia-style video questions, personal progress, and recognition-only rankings. It does not offer wagering or real-money gambling.

What families should know

  • The app is completely free, with no purchase or payment required.
  • XP is awarded only for correct football predictions and correct video-question or player-guess answers.
  • XP has no monetary value, cannot be bought, transferred, or redeemed, and unlocks nothing.
  • Leaderboards provide recognition only, with no winners, prizes, or rewards.
  • Links may open YouTube or other external websites governed by their own age and privacy rules.

Controls

Users can manage notifications, choose app language, report concerns to support, and permanently delete their account in Settings.
