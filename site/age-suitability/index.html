Age suitability

Designed for fans aged 13+

Abu 3meer contains football predictions, trivia-style video questions, personal progress, and recognition-only rankings. It does not offer wagering or real-money gambling.

What families should know

  • Free features require no purchase. Optional monthly or yearly subscriptions unlock Members content, a subscriber badge, recognition XP for activation and verified renewals, and a multiplier on three types of correct match predictions.
  • XP is also awarded once at signup (50 XP), for the first login each UTC day (5 XP), and for correct football predictions and correct video-question or player-guess answers.
  • XP is not sold as a standalone currency, has no monetary value, cannot be transferred or redeemed, and unlocks nothing.
  • Leaderboards provide recognition only, with no winners, prizes, or rewards.
  • Links may open YouTube or other external websites governed by their own age and privacy rules.

Controls

Users can manage notifications, choose app language, report or block another public profile, contact support, and permanently delete their account in Settings.
