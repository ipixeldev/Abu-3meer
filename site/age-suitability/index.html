Age suitability

Designed for fans aged 13+

Abu 3meer contains football predictions, trivia-style questions, personal progress, and rankings. It does not offer wagering or real-money gambling.

What families should know

  • No user pays or stakes money to submit a prediction.
  • An incorrect answer does not cause a financial loss.
  • In-app points have no cash value.
  • Links may open YouTube or other external websites governed by their own age and privacy rules.
  • Real-world promotions, if any, have separate eligibility and age rules and are void where prohibited.

Controls

Users can manage notifications, choose app language, report concerns to support, and permanently delete their account in Settings.
