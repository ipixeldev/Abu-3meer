Account Deletion

You can delete your account inside the Abu 3meer app from Settings. If you cannot access the app, email support from the address connected to your account.

YouTube Membership Verification

If you signed in with email or Apple, link your Google account in Settings to verify your channel membership while keeping the same Abu 3meer account.
